Daybreak

A calendar with tasks and reminders, and a personal feed: Claude reads your weather, local news, email and calendar and tells you what matters to you today. Tell it what to focus on, and it learns.

  • Calendar with day, week and month views, repeating events and reminders; imports and exports standard .ics files.
  • Tasks with deadlines, and notes or reminders without one.
  • The feed uses Claude with your own API key. You choose how often it updates (each update costs a little) and when it notifies you.
  • Email is read-only: Gmail (with an app password), other IMAP mail, or Outlook.
  • Optional: keep your Mac and phone in sync through your own Cloudflare R2 storage, encrypted so only your devices can read it.

Mac

Download for Mac
Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 13 or newer
  • Open the downloaded file and drag Daybreak into Applications.
  • The first time you open it, macOS says it can't check the developer. Click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. This is needed only once.
  • Updates: Daybreak checks for new versions and offers them in the app (Settings → Updates).

Android

Download for Android
Android 10 or newer
  • Allow your browser to install apps when Android asks, then open the downloaded file.
  • Updates: Daybreak checks this page and offers new versions in the app (Settings → Updates).
